# AI Speaking Buddy — Development Phases Index

> **Product Vision:** Build a living, intelligent English-speaking companion that behaves like a real friend, listens carefully, understands thoughts, responds naturally, and continuously helps users improve their English.

## Three Core Intelligence Layers

1. **Conversation Intelligence** — Listening, understanding context, and responding like a real friend.
2. **English Intelligence** — Identifying mistakes, distinguishing grammar vs. naturalness, explaining rules, and teaching better phrasing.
3. **Learning Intelligence** — Remembering conversation context, tracking recurring weaknesses, and measuring improvement over time.

## Five Initial Delivery Milestones

1. **01 — A Working Voice Companion:** Listens, understands, speaks, and holds natural conversations.
2. **02 — A Reliable English Correction Engine:** Accurately detects mistakes and explains how to fix them.
3. **03 — A Personalized Learning System:** Remembers mistakes, preferences, and learning progress.
4. **04 — A Complete Learning Dashboard:** Tracks grammar, vocabulary, fluency, and weekly improvement.
5. **05 — A Living AI Personality:** Orb, voice, expressions, and reactions make the companion feel alive.

> **Architectural Rule:** Keep the **AI Conversation Engine**, **Grammar Engine**, and **Learning-Memory Engine** decoupled behind well-defined structured APIs so speech or LLM models can be upgraded independently without rebuilding the app.
