## **3. Important IELTS lesson: Not every matching word is a synonym**

Look at these examples:

**Example 1:**

- Question: *Populations will fall.*
- Passage: *Undergo a decline.*

Here, the words are different, but the meaning is almost identical.

- Fall = අඩු වීම
- Decline = අඩු වීම

# **2. IELTS Reading – Question wording and passage paraphrases**

This is especially important for IELTS Reading.

The question and passage often communicate the same meaning using different words. They do not always use identical spelling or pronunciation.

For example:

- **False assumptions** → **Contrary to popular belief**
- **Others also procrastinate** → **We're not the first person to procrastinate, nor the last**
- **Salaries are lower** → **Annual income decreased**

## **5. Particularly useful IELTS Reading linking words and connectors**

| Linking word / phrase | Sinhala meaning                             | How it is used + example and Sinhala translation                                                                                                                       |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| And                   | සහ / තවද                                    | Adds information. *She studied art and became a teacher.* — ඇය කලාව හැදෑරූ අතර ගුරුවරියක් බවට පත් වූවාය.                                                               |
| Also                  | එසේම / තවද                                  | Adds another point. *She also travelled abroad.* — ඇය විදේශගතවද සංචාරය කළාය.                                                                                           |
| In addition to        | ...ට අමතරව                                  | Adds another item. *In addition to roads, the city built pumps.* — මාර්ගවලට අමතරව නගරය පොම්පද ඉදි කළේය.                                                                |
| Furthermore           | තවද / එයට අමතරව                             | Adds a supporting point, usually formal. *Furthermore, the project created jobs.* — තවද එම ව්‍යාපෘතිය රැකියා ඇති කළේය.                                                 |
| Moreover              | එපමණක් නොව / තවද                            | Adds a stronger or important point. *Moreover, the method is inexpensive.* — එපමණක් නොව එම ක්‍රමය අඩු වියදම් සහිතය.                                                    |
| Additionally          | අමතරව                                       | Adds information. *Additionally, farmers received training.* — අමතරව ගොවීන්ට පුහුණුව ලබා දෙන ලදී.                                                                      |
| As well as            | ... මෙන්ම                                   | Connects two things. *She painted flowers as well as landscapes.* — ඇය භූ දර්ශන මෙන්ම මල්ද සිතුවම් කළාය.                                                               |

| Above all             | සියල්ලටම වඩා                                | Emphasises the most important thing. *Above all, safety must be considered.* — සියල්ලටම වඩා ආරක්ෂාව සලකා බැලිය යුතුය.                                                  |
| At least              | අවම වශයෙන්                                  | Shows a minimum amount. *At least 50 people attended.* — අවම වශයෙන් පුද්ගලයන් 50 දෙනෙක් සහභාගී වූහ.                                                                    |
| At most               | උපරිම වශයෙන්                                | Shows a maximum amount. *The journey takes at most two hours.* — ගමනට උපරිම වශයෙන් පැය දෙකක් ගත වේ.                                                                    |
| Rather                | තරමක් / ඒ වෙනුවට (සන්දර්භය අනුව)            | Can show preference or correction. *It was not a failure, but rather a learning experience.* — එය අසාර්ථකත්වයක් නොව, ඉගෙනීමේ අත්දැකීමක් විය.                           |
| Notably               | විශේෂයෙන් සඳහන් කළ යුතු ලෙස                 | Highlights an important detail. *Notably, the number of attacks declined.* — විශේෂයෙන් සඳහන් කළ යුතු කරුණක් වන්නේ ප්‍රහාර සංඛ්‍යාව අඩු වීමයි.                          |
| In other respects     | අනෙකුත් අංශවලින්                            | Shows differences or similarities in other aspects. *The two methods are similar in other respects.* — අනෙකුත් අංශවලින් ක්‍රම දෙක සමාන වේ.                             |
| To some extent        | යම් ප්‍රමාණයකට                              | Shows a limited degree. *The new method helped to some extent.* — නව ක්‍රමය යම් ප්‍රමාණයකට උපකාරී විය.                                                                 |
| In terms of           | ... සම්බන්ධයෙන් / ... අනුව                  | Introduces the aspect being discussed. *The method is effective in terms of cost.* — වියදම සම්බන්ධයෙන් එම ක්‍රමය ඵලදායීය.                                              |

## **6. Important IELTS Reading tips: how linking words help you find answers**

| Linking word                           | What you should look for in the passage                                     |
| -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| However / But / Nevertheless           | The writer may introduce an opposite idea or correct a previous assumption. |
| Although / Despite / In spite of       | Look for two contrasting facts.                                             |
| Because / Since / Due to               | Look for the reason or cause.                                               |
| Therefore / Consequently / Thus        | Look for the result or effect.                                              |
| For example / Such as / For instance   | An example that supports a general statement.                               |
| In other words / That is               | A paraphrase or explanation of the previous sentence.                       |
| Similarly / Likewise                   | A similar situation or comparison.                                          |
| Whereas / While / Unlike               | A difference between two groups, things or situations.                      |
| In addition / Moreover / Furthermore   | Additional supporting information.                                          |
| Instead / Rather than                  | An alternative action or idea.                                              |
| Eventually / Subsequently / Afterwards | An event that happens later.                                                |
| In particular / Specifically           | A more precise detail about something.                                      |
| In spite of / Even though              | A result that happens despite a difficulty.                                 |
| Unless / Provided that                 | A condition that must be satisfied.                                         |
